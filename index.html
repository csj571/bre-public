What is not claimed

This is detection, not prediction — every flag lands on or after the onset, and nothing here is a trading signal. None of the mathematics is novel (kriging 1950s, Kalman 1960, Brier 1950, BOCPD 2007, BALD 2011); the contribution is a faithful, inspectable synthesis with external validation attached. Validation transfers only where ground truth is cheap, so "validated on VIX and the S&P" means validated there — not everywhere the same machinery runs. The detector is also chatty: it draws 5–6 flags a year in calm markets, and a shuffled placebo fires more often than real data. The supported claim is fast detection of calm→turbulent transitions, not low-false-alarm event detection.
